Towards Explainable Neural Networks

Springer | Oct 2025
Explainable AI CNN Computer Vision
Springer | Oct 2025

To address the 'black box' nature of clinical CNN models, I pivoted my research focus towards Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI). I implemented a custom Layer-wise Relevance Propagation (LRP) mechanism integrated with the VGG16 architecture.

Through this, I successfully generated pixel-level visualizations that explicitly highlighted the specific regions contributing most to the model's predictions. I also integrated real-time video preprocessing to ensure greater transparency in these models. Ultimately, I aimed to create effective, transparent AI systems for high-stakes sectors like healthcare.

Tech Stack & Concepts: Explainable AI (XAI), Layer-wise Relevance Propagation (LRP), VGG16 Architecture, Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Computer Vision.

Lung Cancer Detection Using Medical Image Processing

Springer | Oct 2025
Medical Imaging ResNet50 DenseNet201
Springer | Oct 2025

Focusing on healthcare AI, I developed an EfficientNetB3 model specifically tailored for detecting lung cancer from CT scans. I rigorously benchmarked the performance of this model against a hybrid ResNet50-DenseNet201 architecture.

My proposed model achieved a superior classification accuracy of 94.29 percent, significantly enhancing early clinical detection capabilities. This work underlined the substantial potential of deep learning methodologies I applied in providing earlier and more reliable disease diagnoses.

Tech Stack & Concepts: Deep Learning, EfficientNetB3, ResNet50, DenseNet201, Medical Image Processing (CT Scans), Classification Benchmarking.

Leveraging Swin Transformer for Robust Object Detection

IEEE | Jul 2025
Autonomous Vehicles Swin Transformer Object Detection
IEEE | Jul 2025

In this independent research initiative, I tested how advanced architectures hold up under real-world, challenging conditions. I rigorously compared transformer-based models against traditional CNN object detectors in the context of autonomous driving.

I was responsible for benchmarking critical performance metrics, including accuracy, robustness, and real-time efficiency. Through my experimental design and in-depth model analysis, I established that Swin Transformers consistently outperformed traditional models when subjected to noise, motion blur, and harsh weather distortions.

Tech Stack & Concepts: Swin Transformers, Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Object Detection, Computer Vision, Autonomous Systems, Performance Benchmarking.

Transforming College Counseling

IEEE | Mar 2023
Web Dev Mobile App UI/UX
IEEE | Mar 2023

After my university surveys revealed that eighty percent of students were unaware of on-campus mental health services, I launched this initiative to bridge the gap between awareness and action. By gathering qualitative feedback directly from counselors and students, I designed and developed an intuitive web and mobile application.

I built the platform to enable students to easily book counseling sessions. To foster a safe and judgment-free environment, I incorporated features for anonymous story sharing, integrated podcasts, and motivational resources into the application.

Tech Stack & Concepts: Web Development, Mobile Application Development, UI/UX, User-Centered Design, Qualitative Data Integration.
